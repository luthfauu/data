# Retail Store Performance Analysis

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail store performance to identify the key factors influencing monthly sales revenue.  
The goal is to understand what differentiates high-performing stores from underperforming ones and convert the findings into practical business insights.

The analysis explores operational, marketing, customer, and external variables such as product variety, store size, marketing spend, employee efficiency, customer footfall, promotions, and economic conditions.

---

## Objectives

- Identify the strongest drivers of store revenue  
- Evaluate effectiveness of marketing and promotions  
- Understand customer behavior vs actual sales conversion  
- Detect hidden inefficiencies in store operations  
- Generate actionable recommendations for improving sales  

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Python  
- Pandas & NumPy  
- Matplotlib & Seaborn  
- Jupyter Notebook  

---

## Dataset Features

The dataset includes variables such as:

Product Variety, Customer Footfall, Store Size & Age, Employee Efficiency, Marketing Spend & Promotions, Competitor Distance, Economic Indicator, Store Location & Category and Monthly Sales Revenue (Target Variable)

---

## Analysis Performed

- Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- Feature engineering (Size Bands, Marketing Bands)  
- Univariate, bivariate, and multivariate visual analysis  
- Correlation and relationship analysis  
- Pivot and filtering based investigations  
- Business-focused insight extraction  

---

## Key Insights

- Product variety and store size are the strongest drivers of revenue  
- Customer footfall alone does not guarantee higher sales  
- Marketing spend and promotions move together but do not strongly impact revenue  
- Some stores show high efficiency or high visitors but still low revenue, indicating conversion issues  
- External factors such as economic indicator and competitor distance show minimal influence on sales  

---

## Business Recommendations

- Expand product assortment in smaller stores  
- Focus on improving sales conversion rather than only increasing visitors  
- Review pricing, display strategy, and product placement  
- Re-evaluate marketing ROI instead of increasing spending blindly  
- Investigate high-efficiency but low-revenue stores for operational issues

This project demonstrates end-to-end exploratory data analysis, business thinking, and insight generation suitable for entry-level Data Analyst roles.
